DIAGRAM: 40-Year Federal Data Evolution Timeline
ASPECT RATIO: 16:9 (1920w × 1080h logical units)
STYLE: Minimalist presentation figure. Dark background, light elements. Serif font (Georgia). No decorative elements.

=== LAYOUT ===

Dark navy (#112E51) background fills the entire canvas.

ONE single white line (#FFFFFF, 3px) curves gently from lower-left to upper-right across the canvas. The line is one continuous stroke that never lifts, never retraces, never touches itself. General movement is always lower-left toward upper-right. Gentle S-curves only.

The line carries 10 milestones, then ends. After the final milestone, at least 25% of the canvas is empty navy.

=== MILESTONES ===

Each milestone sits on the path with:
- A small geometric icon (~40px), colored per palette below
- Year label (bold serif, white)
- Name label (serif, milestone color)

Milestones appear in EXACTLY this order along the path, from start to end. The chronological sequence must not be rearranged:

=== DO NOT ===

- No title, legend, or annotations
- No gradients, shadows, 3D, or textures
- No road markings or lane lines
- No objects after the final "?" — empty canvas only
- No enclosed areas in the path
- Path never reverses direction
- Icons are geometric shapes only
- All text must be readable at presentation scale — no text smaller than the year labels
- Do not render pixel size numbers (like "9px") as visible text
- Do not change any year numbers — they are exact
- Do not change any milestone names — spell them exactly as written above
- Do not reorder milestones — the sequence is 1986, 2001, 2011, 2013, 2017, 2022, 2024, 2025, 2026, 2026
- Do not add dots, circles, or nodes on the path at milestone positions
