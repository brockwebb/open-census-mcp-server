## What They're Doing
Creating prompt-response pairs to assess LLM accuracy, relevancy, and explainability on federal statistical data. Building a tool to evaluate LLM performance AND provide insight into how well federal data assets are structured to support LLM interaction — highlighting opportunities to improve metadata quality, accessibility, and machine-readability.

## What They're NOT Doing
- No tool augmentation (single-shot prompts on base models)
- No fitness-for-use judgment layer
- No expert judgment delivery at point of reasoning
- No auditability pipeline
- Focus is measurement, not intervention
