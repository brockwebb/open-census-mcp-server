## 1. Readability Scorer

### Purpose

Reports Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level and word count for each assembled paper section. FK Grade approximates the U.S. school grade required to read the text comfortably. Target range for academic research papers: **12–16** (college level). Values above 16 suggest overly complex syntax; below 12 may indicate overly simplified prose for the venue.

### Usage

```bash
cd /Users/brock/Documents/GitHub/census-mcp-server
pip install textstat  # one-time

python3 editing_tools/readability_scores.py
```

**Note on 05_results.md:** The low FK score (9.9) is expected. Results sections dominated by tables, numbers, and short declarative sentences score artificially low. Not a prose quality concern.

**Note on 07_limitations_future.md:** FK 16.1 is at the upper edge. The italic run-in head structure adds short sentences which may pull it up; re-score after further prose edits.

### Interpretation

- FK Grade 12–14: Standard academic prose, accessible
- FK Grade 14–16: Dense academic prose, normal for methodology/theory sections
- FK Grade > 16: Consider sentence-length reduction or splitting complex sentences
- FK Grade < 10: May indicate table-heavy sections (acceptable) or oversimplified prose

### Known Limitations

- `textstat.flesch_kincaid_grade()` does not strip Markdown syntax, LaTeX inline math, or Quarto figure/table directives. These add noise but are consistent across runs, making relative comparisons valid.
- Word count includes Markdown tokens (`##`, `**`, `@citation`). Treat as an approximation.
- Results sections with dense tables will always score low; use the score directionally, not absolutely.

## 2. Compliance Audit

See `paper/audit_report.md` (regenerated by `paper/compliance_audit.sh`).

Checks:
1. Em dashes (U+2014)
2. Double-hyphen dashes (` -- `)
3. "novel" (banned word)
4. "hallucination/hallucinate" (use "confabulation")
5. Vocabulary drift: "pragmatic item(s)", "pragmatic context item(s)", "context node(s)"
6. Bold pseudo-headers in running prose
7. Bullet points in prose sections (01–08)
8. Stale sidecar files
9. Stale `draft_v1.md`
